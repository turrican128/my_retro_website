11 October: the street got depth, a hidden bug came out, a release went out at 19:31, and an hour later the fights got rougher.

{icon:joystick}07:42 · walking in depth

Fighters now walk up and down the street, not only left and right, and fire on its own punches. A blow lands only within 3 units of depth, 6 screen lines.

{icon:bug}The frozen enemies

Depth exposed an old bug: the enemies had been half frozen. Once it was fixed, their pose changes went from 1,328 to 2,679 per 10,000 frames. In other words, every fight measurement until then had been taken against half-frozen enemies. Fights are about twice as busy now.

{icon:fist}The same evening

Then four more, after the release:

  • Enemies line up on your line before they attack. One parked 12 units off the line made 0 attacks in 800 frames.
  • Knock-downs: a flying kick, a sweep, or a third hit within 50 frames puts a fighter down for about 2.5 seconds. Everyone gets up, and nobody gets juggled.
  • Four basic moves on the stick (punch, kick, jump kick, back kick), all 9 stick combinations checked.
  • CharPad 3.5+ maps, tested on all 214 of CharPad 3.80's example maps, plus the level rules for the artist.
