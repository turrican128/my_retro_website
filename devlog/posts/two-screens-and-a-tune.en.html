The first title screen wasn't to my taste, so we started over from a screen I love: Double Dragon II!! Yeah... Two screens that swap, a logo and then the credits, and the music never stops for a moment.

{icon:star}First screen

FIST III, in the 4x4 font: each letter is four characters wide and four tall, and the letters share characters, so the whole logo costs very little. I picked the colours from six palettes drawn exactly the way the C64 displays them.

I went with an "ice" style: blue and light blue, with highlights in white and Cyan.

Under the logo, The Revenge in red handwriting, a nod to the title screen of Double Dragon II on the C64 (remember that?), where the lettering is taken from pixel for pixel. Its four character rows are hires, not multicolour, so the thin pixel lines of the handwriting stay sharp. The whole logo moved up one row to make room for it, and the fighters didn't move.

Below the logo, two fighters. These are our game's sprites, turned into characters by a small tool, so the picture is exactly the fighters you play with. And at the bottom, PLAYER 1 USES JOYSTICK PORT 2, with the joystick words glowing and fading along the C64's brightness steps, one step every 3 frames.

{icon:map}Two screens, one map

Both screens sit in one map, 80 columns wide. The second screen starts at column 40, so switching is just a 40-column jump, with our fade in between. Nothing gets loaded, so the music doesn't stutter. The first screen stays up for about 10 seconds, the credits for 6. Pressing fire starts the game at any moment.

{icon:note}A tune for now

Until we have music of our own, the intro plays Hi-t. Soul by the musician Link, from 1990. It was moved to the game's music address, $F400, with our relocation tool, which plays the original and the copy side by side and compares every SID write: 6000 frames, not one different.

{icon:chip}Behind the scenes

And when I opened the Title screen in CharPad, everything was in one colour. One wrong number in the file: the character colour was 1, meaning hires. Multicolour needs 8 to 15. Now it's 9.
