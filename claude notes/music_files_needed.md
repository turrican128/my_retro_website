# Music Files Needed — Kleiner'82 Player

The `docs/kleiner82.html` page has a built-in audio player.
Tracks marked with `highlight: true` have a `file:` path and are **playable** — these MP3s must exist in the repo.
Tracks **without** a `file:` path are display-only (no play button shown).

All files go under: `docs/assets/music/<album-folder>/`
