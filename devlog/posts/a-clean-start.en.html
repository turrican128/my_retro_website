A short one this time. The graphics and the music aren't in yet, so we closed two small things that were waiting on the list. You see one the moment the game starts; the other only shows in the tests.

{icon:eye}A clean title screen

Until now, the top-left corner of the title screen, above the FIST III logo, showed a score of "0", a small head and the number 3. Those are a new game's score and lives, and they showed up before anyone pressed fire. Now the corner is empty, and they come back the moment you press fire, or when the demo starts playing by itself. The HI-SCORE on the right stays, because on a title screen it actually belongs.

{icon:bug}A test that failed for no reason

The boss has a rule: a single flying kick or a single sweep doesn't knock him down. Only a combo or an emptied energy bar does. We have an automatic test in VICE that's supposed to check this, and it had been failing ever since we moved to seven basic moves. The boss wasn't broken. The test still pressed up and fire to throw a flying kick, but today that's the high kick, and the player has no flying kick at all anymore. So the test was checking something that can't happen.

Now the test itself turns the player's first blow into a flying kick, and so checks the real rule. And to know it can really fail, we also ran it once with the boss's protection switched off. Three single kicks, three knockdowns, and the test failed, just as it should.

A brief for the musician and a small checker for the .sid file, so when the music arrives we know within seconds whether it fits in memory. And the turning glitch from the game-night post is still on the list. As soon as we can reproduce it, we'll catch it.
