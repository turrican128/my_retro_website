A short post this time. The graphics and music haven't arrived yet, so we closed two small things that were waiting on the list. One you see as soon as the game loads; the other only shows up in testing.

{icon:eye}A clean title screen

Until now, the top-left corner of the title screen, above the FIST III logo, held a score of "0", a small head and the number 3. That's the score and lives of a new game, and they showed up before anyone had pressed fire. Now the corner is empty
, and they come back the moment you press fire, or when the demo starts playing by itself. The HI-SCORE on the right stays, because on a title screen it actually belongs.

{icon:bug}A test that failed for no reason

The boss has a rule: a single flying kick or a single sweep doesn't knock him down. Only a combo or an empty energy bar does. We have an automated test in VICE that's supposed to check this, and it had been failing ever since we moved to seven basic moves. The boss wasn't broken. The test still pressed up and fire to do a flying kick, but today that's the high kick, and the player doesn't have a flying kick at all any more. So the test was checking something that can't happen.

Now the test itself turns the player's first hit into a flying kick, so it checks the real rule. And to know it can actually fail, we also ran it once with the boss's protection switched off. Three single kicks, three knockdowns, and the test failed the way it should.

{icon:page}What's next

A help page for the musician and a small checker for the .sid file, so that when the music arrives we know within seconds whether it fits in memory. And the turning glitch from the playtest-evening post is still on the list. As soon as we can reproduce it, we'll catch it!!

That's it for this time.. a bit short, but sometimes a small fix or a single PR makes a big difference to the game...
