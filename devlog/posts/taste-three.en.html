Taste #3 is up on itch.io. New music by Mythus with a song for the boss, a real ending, and a boss you can actually beat. And on the way we found a bug that made the first level repeat itself.

{icon:note}Three songs

In the last post I wrote that the next thing was the level music. Well, it's here, and then some. Mythus sent a version with three songs: one for the title screen, one for the levels, and one for the boss. The moment the boss comes on screen the music changes, and when he goes down it's back to the level song.

The level song and the boss song use only two channels. That leaves the SID's third channel to the sound effects alone, so the hits no longer cut into the tune.

About the effects: last time I wrote that there were some sounds I really didn't like. So we built a page that plays every effect, and I picked what stays and what goes. Seven stayed, five went. Now every blow that lands has one sound, and the boss has a heavy one of his own.

{icon:star}A real ending

Until now, finishing the third level got you GAME COMPLETE on one line and back to the title screen. Pretty sad after three bosses. Now there are fireworks in the sky, CONGRATULATIONS in rainbow colours, and then the fighter walks on his own through all three levels, with no enemies, while the credits are typed letter by letter on the top line. At the end comes THANKS FOR PLAYING.

The credits needed letters the top line's font didn't have, like J and Y. We drew them in the characters of Fist II's old HUD, which our game no longer shows.

{icon:fist}A boss you can beat

The boss was simply too strong. He had three full energy bars, about eight times a normal enemy, and he blocks almost every blow. We cut him to two shorter bars, about half of what he had. He's still tough, but now it's a fight, not a punishment.

{icon:map}Back to the ladder

The train and the depot have a ladder up to the roof. But whoever reached the right end of the roof was stuck facing a wall, with no hint at all. Now, as you get near the end, the top line says GO BACK TO THE LADDER.

{icon:bug}The level that repeated itself

In the tour build (the one without enemies) I reached the end of the first level, and it started again. It took a while to see why. I play in VICE with a joystick on the keyboard: the arrow keys and Right Ctrl. And on the C64 the joystick and the keyboard share the same lines. So walking right with the arrow keys reads exactly like pressing L, the key that skips to the next level in the test build. The game skipped levels by itself, and after the last one it went back to the start.

Now L isn't read while the joystick moves, and in the released game it doesn't exist at all. And another bug I wrote about last time, the title screen starting a game by itself after GAME COMPLETE? It was never in the game. It was in our test tool.

{icon:joystick}Download and play

Taste #3 is on the game's page on itch.io: a d64 disk with dir art and a prg file, joystick in port 2, a PAL machine. The fighters are still Fist II's original sprites until our artist's sprites go in. The levels are already ours.

{icon:page}What's next

The fighters' new sprites. And for testing there's now also a build with unlimited lives, so every level can be played to the end and the fights checked without a GAME OVER.
