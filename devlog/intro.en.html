Devlog

Building a C64 beat'em-up on the engine of Fist II: The Legend Continues (Beam Software / Melbourne House, 1986). It started on 6 October 2026 by taking the original apart: an exact rebuild from the tape, then a rewrite in my own style, and a game of our own on top of it. Where it's heading has a working title: Fist III: The Revenge.
