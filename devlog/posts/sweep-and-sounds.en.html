Taster 4 is up on itch.io. A week of small things you hear and feel: new sounds for the hits!!, an old Fist II move back in the player's hands, and a hunt for the bugs that were hiding in the game.

{icon:note}New sounds for the hits

Our musician Rock/Finnish Gold sent a new set of effects, each in its own SID file: a punch, a kick, a longer punch for the boss, a knockout when an enemy goes down, and a body slamming into the floor. A SID file carries its own small player, and that player can't live in the game next to the music. So we play each file in an emulator, record what it does to the SID chip frame by frame, and turn that into data for the effects player the game already has. The sound comes out the same, now on the SID's third channel.

A small lesson along the way: going by the numbers, I said the kick was exactly the same sound as the punch. Only one bit was different. But that bit decides whether the note in the middle of the sound jumps out or stays buried in the decay, and to the ear the difference is obvious. The ear won. Then a second kick arrived, with a bit of vibrato, and that's the one in the game now.

{icon:fist}The sweep is back

In Fist II, down and fire was the sweep: the fighter drops low and swings a kick along the floor. When we simplified the controls a few weeks ago, down and fire became just another punch. Now the sweep is back. The move never really left the game, because the enemies kept using it, so bringing it back cost nothing in memory.

As in the original, the fighter crouches first and only then kicks, so you hold down and fire until the leg comes out. A sweep that lands floors the enemy in one hit. But the enemies know it too: they see the crouch coming and jump right over it. You have to pick your moment.

{icon:joystick}Download and play

Taster 4 is on the game's page on itch.io: a d64 disk with dir art and a prg file, joystick in port 2, PAL machine. Down and fire is the sweep: hold until the leg comes out. The fighters are still the original Fist II sprites until our artist's sprites go in.

{icon:page}What's next

A real sound for the moment the player takes a hit (for now it borrows the boss's punch), and enemies that aren't all the same: different colours, different fighting styles, more or less energy. Still plenty of work to do!!
