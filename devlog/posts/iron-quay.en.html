A big day for the game! We have a brand-new first level, Iron Quay, drawn for the project by a good friend of mine. Along the way the Fist II ladders came back, and we found and fixed two bugs that had been sitting there quietly.

{icon:star}Iron Quay

A night street at the harbour: lit windows, an arcade, a dojo, a bar, fire escapes, and a loading dock at the far end, by the water. It's all original art, with no graphics taken from other games. The level is as long as the Fist II level, 816 columns and four rooms, and it uses 212 of the 216 characters a level gets. My friend sent a plain CharPad file, and our tool turned it into exactly the same bytes, byte for byte.

From today Iron Quay is the first level in the game, and the Fist II maps come after it.

{icon:joystick}Fighters and a boss

Five enemies wait along the street, each a little harder than the last, and the boss waits at the end of the docks. On the first try the boss simply vanished. We had put him too close to the left wall of the last room, which is narrow, the hits knocked him off the screen, and the game deletes any enemy that leaves the screen. No boss, no end to the level. We moved him to the end of the docks, and now the fight works: two energy bars, a knockdown after each bar, and only once he's beaten does the exit open to the next level.

{icon:chip}A corner of memory nobody had used

The levels are packed into a bank in memory, and the bank has two areas: one in the middle of memory, and one under the C64's chips, at $d000. With Iron Quay the first area filled up, and the title screen moved to the second area, for the first time ever. One check in the game's build was too strict there and stopped everything, even though nothing overlapped. We fixed it, and checked in VICE that the title screen comes up from there as it should.

{icon:bug}Ladders, and a bug that dragged the player

We brought back the Fist II climbing: a level can have a roof, with a ladder leading up to it. Walk to the wall by the ladder, keep pushing up, and you climb. The second level already has a ladder like that, with a roof. The climbing animation still needs work, and it's on the list.

Along the way we found a bug that had been in the game for a long time: when the screen scrolled after the player, walking straight up or down dragged him sideways, almost 18 columns in three seconds. Without the fix you couldn't stop next to a ladder. Now the picture stays put when you walk up and down.

{icon:page}What's next

A better climbing animation, more enemies at the docks, and the level music from Mythus. All 12 of our VICE tests pass, including a full game through all three levels and back to the title screen.

That's it for this time.. thanks to my good friend for the harbour haha, now there's somewhere to fight!!
