Devlog

Building a C64 beat'em-up on the engine of Fist II: The Legend Continues (Beam Software / Melbourne House, 1986). It started on 6 October 2026 by taking the original apart: first an exact rebuild from the tape, then a rewrite in my own style, and on top of that a game of our own. Where it's heading already has a working title: Fist III: The Revenge. I honestly don't know yet whether I'll redevelop the whole game on top of the Reverse Engineering, but we'll see. For now I'm using AI models quite a lot to improve the game's engine and add new things to it, such as a new graphics map for every level, new music with Sound Effects, more enemy characters, and a "boss" stage at the end of every level. I take inspiration from two games I really love. The first is the legendary Double Dragon, and the second is Crime Fighters, a great game from Konami. I hope you enjoy the devlog of my game.
