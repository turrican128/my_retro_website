RETRO
8bitretro.tech

I'm Alex Goldblat, 51, from Herzliya - this is my retro home and personal corner of the web.

For as long as I can remember, I've lived and breathed the retro world: the Commodore 64 was the first machine I fell in love with, and I still build games, demos, and new experiences on it, powered by the magic of 8-bit.

I'm also a musician, creating as part of the project Kleiner'82 - synthwave music with SID touches, Paula vibes, and a true '80s feel. I released a cover album of my favorite Commodore 64 tunes, and here on the site you'll find both original tracks and special arrangements.

I love the 8-bit and 16-bit worlds, and especially the culture around them - colors, style, graphics, music, and atmosphere just like back then.
