A big day for the game! We have a brand-new first level, Iron Quay, drawn by a good friend of the project. On the way Fist II's ladders came back, and we found and fixed two bugs that had been waiting quietly.

{icon:star}Iron Quay

A night street at the harbour: lit windows, an arcade, a dojo, a bar, fire escapes and a loading bay at the end, by the water. All of it is original art, no graphics from other games. The level is as long as Fist II's, 816 columns, four rooms, and it uses 212 of the 216 characters a level has. Our friend sent an ordinary CharPad file, and our tool built exactly the same bytes from it, byte for byte.

From today Iron Quay is the game's first level, and Fist II's maps come after it.

{icon:joystick}Fighters and a boss

Five enemies wait along the street, each a little tougher than the last, and the boss waits at the harbour's end. On the first try the boss simply vanished: we had put him too close to the left wall of the last room, which is narrow, the blows threw him off the screen, and the game removes an enemy who leaves the screen. Without a boss the level never ends. We moved him to the harbour's end, and now the fight works: two energy bars, a knockdown after each one, and only once he is beaten does the exit open to the next level.

{icon:chip}A corner of memory nobody had used

The levels are packed into a bank in memory, and it has two regions: one in the middle of memory, and one under the C64's chips, at $d000. With Iron Quay the first region filled up, and the title screen moved to the second one, for the first time ever. One check in the game's build was too strict there and stopped everything, even though nothing overlapped. We fixed it, and checked in VICE that the title screen loads from there as it should.

{icon:bug}Ladders, and a bug that dragged the player

We brought Fist II's climbing back: a level can have a roof, and a ladder leads up to it. Walk to the wall by the ladder, keep pushing up, and you climb. The second level already has a ladder like that with a roof. The climbing animation still needs work, and it's on the list.

Along the way we found a bug that had been in the game for a long time: while the view was following the player, walking straight up or down dragged him sideways, almost 18 columns in three seconds. Without the fix you couldn't stop next to a ladder. Now the picture stays put when you walk up and down.

{icon:page}What's next

A better climbing animation, more enemies on the quay, and the levels' music from Mythus. All 12 of our tests in VICE pass, including a whole game through the three levels and back to the title screen.

That's it for this time.. thanks to our good friend for the quay, now there's somewhere to fight!!
